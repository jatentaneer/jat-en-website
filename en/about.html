About Us

Because our customers' voices are the first blueprint of our company

Where We Began

J.A. Tech Entaneer grew out of hands-on experience in the filter and engineering industry — expertise built over years of real work on production lines, backed by international training including EHEDG (European Hygienic Engineering & Design Group), the European standard for hygienic design in the food industry.

One lesson was always clear along the way: off-the-shelf products cannot meet the real needs of every plant. Every production line has its own requirements, and customers deserve what fits their work — not just what happens to be in stock.

"We want someone who truly understands our work."

The Turning Point

The real drive came from a voice heard again and again on-site — customers didn't just want a salesperson, but someone who understood what each production line needed, and who was close enough to support them. J.A. Tech Entaneer Co., Ltd. was founded on 8 February 2025 with a simple but firm mission: to carry forward hard-earned expertise, from a home base in Thailand toward international standards.

Today and Beyond

We provide full-service support primarily to the food industry, and are expanding our expertise into other sectors such as petrochemicals for long-term business sustainability — from high-quality filter manufacturing and engineering design to installation and maintenance of production equipment. Our customers trust us for one core strength: fast response and on-time delivery.

Our company may be young, but our expertise has traveled much further.
