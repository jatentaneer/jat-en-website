Our Products

High-quality industrial filters for every application

Sintered Filter

Multi-layer sintered wire mesh filters — strong, resistant to high pressure and temperature, cleanable and reusable. Built for precision filtration with a long service life.

Filter Housing

Housings for filter elements, designed and built to match each site's pipe size, flow rate, and working pressure. Available in stainless steel and special materials on request.

Inline Filter

Filters installed directly in the pipeline, filtering fluids in-line within the system. Space-saving, easy to remove, clean, and maintain.

Basket Strainer

Basket strainers that catch foreign particles in the pipeline, protecting pumps, valves, and downstream equipment. Available in multiple sizes and filtration grades.

Disk Filter

Disk filters for fine filtration, produced in multiple sizes and filtration grades to suit each system.
